Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 June 2026 · Bug Hunter v__APP_VERSION__

Bug Hunter is a project & bug-tracking app. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and how you can have it deleted. We try to keep this short and plain-language.

1. What we collect

  • Account info — your name, email address, and a one-way hash of your password. You provide these when you sign up.
  • Content you create — projects, bugs, comments, file attachments, custom field values, and saved views.
  • Session info — IP address and User-Agent at the time of sign-in, used to power the "My sessions" screen so you can revoke devices you don't recognise.
  • Audit log — what you did (created a bug, assigned a member, etc.) so administrators in your organisation can review activity.
  • Push notification token — if you install the Android app and grant notification permission, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token so we can notify you about assignments and @mentions. Removed when you sign out.

2. Why we collect it

  • To run the app and let you do what you came here to do.
  • To send notifications you've explicitly opted in to receive.
  • To enforce security (rate-limiting, session integrity, audit trails).

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use your data to train any machine-learning model.

3. Who we share it with

Your data is shared only with the other members of your organisation in Bug Hunter, according to the role permissions set by your admin. Outside of that:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — receives the notification payload and device token so the OS can show the notification on your phone. No other personal data is sent.
  • Our hosting providers (Render for the app server, Neon for the database) — host the data on our behalf. Both are US-based and encrypt data in transit and at rest.

We do not sell or share data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics vendors.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your content as long as your account exists. Audit log rows are retained for one year then automatically purged. When you delete your account (see below), everything is removed within 30 days.

5. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • On the web: https://www.bughunter.co.in/delete-account

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Bugs you reported keep their content (so the rest of your team isn't disrupted) but your name on them is anonymised. If you are the sole admin of an organisation, you'll need to promote another member to admin before you can delete yourself.

6. Your other rights

You can download a JSON copy of everything we have associated with your account at any time: Settings → Account → Export my data. The download is a single JSON file containing your profile, bugs, comments, attachments metadata, sessions, and audit entries.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Sessions use signed cookies with the Secure and SameSite=Lax flags. Mutating requests require a CSRF double-submit token.

8. Children

Bug Hunter is intended for use by adults in professional contexts. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Contact

For any privacy question, contact: privacy@bughunter.co.in.
